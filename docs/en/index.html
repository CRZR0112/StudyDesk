WHAT IT DOES

Not a question bank, your note-organizing desk.

School already gives you enough practice questions. Zhixu solves one problem: turning scattered material into a structure you can revise from.

01

Bring your material in

Image OCR, PDF / DOCX / PPTX / XLSX, or paste text directly. The original material stays available for reference.

02

Turn it into revision structure

Knowledge map, learning objectives, core concepts, key points, formulas, common mistakes, examples, answer frames and flip cards.

03

File it under your subjects

S3–S6, core and elective subjects (including M1 / M2). Notes and settings are stored separately for each account.

POSITIONING

Zhixu does not replace your school’s question bank, and does not sell past papers.

It helps you organize your own notes and gives you search links for related videos.

DATA & PRIVACY

Your notes,
isolated by account.

Accounts, notes, attachments and subject choices are stored in the server database. After signing in, you can only read your own notes; export and account deletion are supported.

If a third-party AI API is used to process material, check the provider’s privacy terms first. Zhixu does not use your notes for training.

HOW IT WORKS

Three steps from input to revision material.

No complicated note-taking system to learn first.

  1. 01

    Choose a subject and topic

    Start from your own subjects, or upload an image or file directly.

  2. 02

    Let Zhixu organize it

    Extract the text and break it into concepts, key points, formulas, common mistakes and flashcards.

  3. 03

    Read, search, revise

    Find notes by subject, form or keyword; jump to related video searches when needed.
